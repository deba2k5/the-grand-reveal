# The Grand Reveal

Yes — that is much better. The game should only be the funny entry mechanism; the reward is a beautiful animated invitation envelope.

🎮 Flow I’d use

Screen 1 — Teaser

HEY YOU. 👀
We have something for you.
But first…
earn your invitation.

[ OPEN THE GAME ]

🕹️ Screen 2 — Tiny funny game

Not a quiz. Something extremely simple and addictive, for example:

“DON'T LET THE IDEA ESCAPE”

A tiny startup/rocket/character moves around the screen.

CATCH IT!
Because good ideas don't wait.

After the player catches it:

MISSION ACCOMPLISHED.
Okay… you earned this.

[ CLAIM MY INVITATION ]

✉️ Screen 3 — Envelope animation

The screen goes dark.

A 3D-style elegant envelope appears in the center.

Text above:

A SPECIAL DELIVERY FOR YOU

The envelope wiggles slightly.

Then:

CLICK TO OPEN

When clicked:

Envelope flap lifts

Letter slides upward

Background particles/confetti appear

Invitation card unfolds

Main message fades in

💌 Actual invitation

Something like:

YOU ARE INVITED

Your presence is not just requested,
it is awaited.

Join us as we bring together
ideas, innovation, ambition & the people crazy enough to build the future.

IEM BENGAL E-SUMMIT

[DATE]
[VENUE]

We'd love to have you there.

Your presence will make this experience even more special.

RSVP →

And a small funny line at the bottom:

P.S. You already completed the game.
So technically, you can't say no now. 😌

✨ Visual style

I would make the invitation itself completely different from the game:

Game: dark, playful, arcade/futuristic
↓
Transition: screen fades + envelope materializes
↓
Invitation: premium black/cream/gold/red, elegant typography, paper texture, cinematic opening animation.

That contrast will make the envelope reveal feel special, rather than just another webpage section.

And the envelope should be the centerpiece, not a normal modal/card. now make the web ui animated one
